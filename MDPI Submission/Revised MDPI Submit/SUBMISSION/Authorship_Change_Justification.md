## Reasons for authorship change

**1. Stage of the editorial process.**

This request is made during peer review, before acceptance and before
publication. The manuscript is currently under major revision following three
reviewer reports. No Correction notice is therefore required, and the request
falls within the period in which the journal's policy states that authorship
changes should be made.

**2. Why the author list changed.**

The reviewers' reports prompted a revision considerably larger than the
editorial pass the authors had anticipated. Two of the methodological concerns
raised were investigated and confirmed, and the affected results were withdrawn
rather than defended. The study was then re-executed from the raw data under
corrected protocols.

The revision also required work that did not exist in the original submission.
The most substantial addition was an external validation of CoinJoin status:
6,269 Bitcoin transactions were retrieved from public block explorers and
tested against published protocol detection rules, confirming 4,491 as CoinJoin
outputs and establishing, for the first time in this study, a measurement
against labels that did not originate in the heuristic being evaluated. A
detection experiment was then built on those confirmed labels, and the
uncertainty on its headline result quantified by bootstrap.

In total the revised manuscript rests on eighteen experiments, each released
with its source code and a version-control commit hash. The manuscript grew
from approximately 10,200 to approximately 19,100 words, and every results
section was rewritten.

## Before submitting

**Only claim what is accurate in section 3.** The journal states that it may
request evidence of authorship and evaluates these requests under COPE
guidance. A specific, modest and verifiable account of each contribution is
more likely to be approved than a broad one, and far easier to support if
queried.

**Check that the Zenodo record matches the claim.** The four are listed there
as research-group contributors. If section 3 claims a different role, do not
cite the record as evidence of it; a mismatch between the two invites the
question rather than settling it.

**Keep this consistent with the Author Contributions statement** in the
manuscript. The two are read together, and a discrepancy between them is the
most common reason these requests are returned.
